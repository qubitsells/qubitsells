# Our Little Pink Day — character and style guide

Scene 1 is the definitive visual reference for the original adult couple.

- Girlfriend: age 22, warm medium-brown skin, warm brown eyes, oval face and shoulder-length wavy dark-brown hair; one small white bow clip on her left side. Oversized plain blush-pink hoodie, white wide-leg trousers, chunky white sneakers with pale-pink details, simple small stud earrings.
- Boyfriend: age 23, warm medium-brown skin, brown eyes, short fluffy tousled black hair, clean-shaven and slightly taller. Oversized creamy-white hoodie, powder-pink straight-leg trousers, white sneakers with blush trim.
- Keep actual reference faces, hair, outfits, footwear and adult proportions throughout.
- Both are young adults in casual clothing. Their affection is expressed through eye contact, held hands, a shared dessert, a little gift and resting together.
- Recurring plush from scene 3: one original white bunny with long soft/floppy ears, pale-pink inner ears, small black eyes and a simple pink nose. Use approved scene 3 or 4 as an extra toy reference when regenerating later shots.
- Original contemporary 2D romance style, clean delicate outlines, soft cel shading, matte pastel backgrounds and airy daylight. Use pink, white and ivory with small natural greenery accents.
- No clothing logos, machine labels, menus, photo captions, text or interface elements in the artwork.
- Supply reference images when regenerating. Text alone cannot guarantee identity consistency. Small generated background, toy or decorative variations can occur; preserve the approved scenes for actual use.

This is a creative direction intended to feel contemporary and playful; audience preference should be tested with real users.
