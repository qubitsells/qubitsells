# Our Little Pink Day

A six-scene original 2D romantic template for a young adult couple, using an airy pink-and-white palette and playful everyday date moments. Created with built-in image generation.

**Little moments. Favorite person.**

## Story order

1. **My favorite meetup** — An ordinary date begins with their favorite person. `01-my-favorite-meetup.png`
2. **One dessert, two spoons** — A shared strawberry dessert becomes their little moment. `02-one-dessert-two-spoons.png`
3. **I won this for you** — He surprises her with a little white bunny plush. `03-i-won-this-for-you.png`
4. **A little photo-booth moment** — They save a silly, happy moment together. `04-a-little-photo-booth-moment.png`
5. **One umbrella for two** — A little rain brings them closer. `05-one-umbrella-for-two.png`
6. **You are my cozy place** — Their day ends with the comfort of being together. `06-you-are-my-cozy-place.png`

## Contents

- Six numbered text-free PNGs.
- `preview.html`: light pink-and-white local sequence viewer. Extract the whole folder before opening.
- `CHARACTERS.md`: adult character, outfit, plush toy and style continuity.
- `PROMPTS.md`: human-readable image and optional animation prompts.
- `image-prompts.json`: complete generation prompts and reference roles.
- `video-prompts.json`: six optional five-second animation prompts; videos have not been generated.
- `sequence.json`: scene order, dimensions, timing, palette and suggested HTML copy.
- `IDE_PROMPT.txt`: copy-ready implementation request for Bonded.

## Size and playback

Native PNGs are 1672x941, approximately 16:9. The preview uses an exact 16:9 viewport with object-fit:cover, without stretching. No resizing or cropping was applied to the PNGs after generation. Suggested edited sequence: five seconds per scene with 500ms overlapping fades, 27.5 seconds total.

## Use

Scene 1 establishes the couple. The later scenes use it as an identity/style reference; small background, decorative and toy variations may occur. Use the approved assets for consistency.

The artwork has no text, signs or labels. Keep personalized names, messages and actions as separate HTML. The final invitation can ask the recipient to choose a dessert date, arcade/photo-booth date or cozy night in. The preview is a local media viewer; real replies and confirmed plans need integration with the app's actual supported flows.
